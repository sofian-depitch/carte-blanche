02The Protocol

5 phases.
End to end.

Phase N°01

Strategic Reframing.

We map who is in the room and what each person needs to say yes.

Output the angle, and the one decision to obtain.

Phase N°02

Narrative Architecture.

We try 2 or 3 storylines and keep the one your audience will follow.

Output the opening, the key idea, the close, then the message of each slide.

Phase N°03

Argument Sequencing.

We put each proof just before the question it answers.

Output the order of your proofs, figures checked.

Phase N°04

Pressure Test.

I ask the hardest questions you could face.

Output your answers, ready.

Phase N°05

Full Simulation.

A timed rehearsal, in real conditions.

Output notes on voice, pace, and pauses. For a live address, notes on your own recording.

04The Signature Test

Every line.
Cross-examined.

The line a skeptic uses against you is often one you wrote.

  1. 01

    Nobody corrects you live.

    Cut every « only » and « first » to what you can prove. Recompute every figure before Finance does.

    Said
    « Nobody in the Group does this yet. »
    A peer answers
    « Nobody? My team launched it last spring. »
  2. 02

    Nobody loses face.

    Say what changed to make it possible now, never how long it sat there.

    Said
    « The demand was always there. »
    The budget holder hears
    « So I missed it. »
  3. 03

    No peer feels ranked.

    Describe the model, never the brand or the tier. Show what yours adds to theirs.

    Said
    « Unlike mass brands, our clients ask for us by name. »
    Heard 2 seats away
    « So mine don’t? »
  4. 04

    You keep control.

    Answer the obvious objection before anyone raises it. Build each part to hold up to a question at slide 3.

    Said
    « We’ll come to the budget at the end. »
    The first interruption
    « Before we go on: what does it cost? »
  5. 05

    Nobody quotes you back.

    Never name the label you deny or the doubt you anticipate. State the fact that rules it out.

    Said
    « This is not a cost-cutting plan. »
    What they remember
    « Cost-cutting. »
  6. 06

    You leave with 1 decision.

    1 ask, small enough to approve today. Credit each partner team before you make it.

    Said
    « We have 3 requests today. »
    The Group CEO answers
    « The first, yes. The other 2, let’s discuss. »
